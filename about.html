Our History

Uganda Martyrs University (UMU) is a private, faith-based university established in 1993 by the Uganda Episcopal Conference. The university is committed to providing quality higher education, research, and training guided by ethical values. Its main campus is located in Nkozi, Uganda.

Our Vision

To be a university that is nationally and internationally recognized for excellence in teaching, learning, research, advancement of knowledge, and community engagement.

Our Mission

To provide quality higher education, training, and research for the betterment of society, guided by ethical values.

Our Core Values

transparancy Accountability Reliability Integrity Quality Action based on institutional ethos and values
